# The Silent Bell

**Promise:** three travellers leave together and return together. The player's objective is always visible and each encounter restores an echo to the journal.

**Route:** meet Elder Sera in Larkspur → collect optional supplies / rest → Old Road dew slimes → bridge and wolves → Sunken Belfry memory spring → wraiths → Bellkeeper → ending.

**Combat rhythm:** Ren acts quickly and finishes weakened enemies; Mira chooses between fire, healing and empowerment; Kael draws attacks and breaks armor. Basic attacks restore MP, allowing a recovering party to keep making progress. Victory gives a small automatic recovery and raises fallen allies. Rest is always available in town.

**Boss:** 650 HP. Above 66%: single-target attacks. Below 66%: area attacks on alternating rounds. Below 30%: amplified area attacks. Guard and healing remain useful throughout.

**Presentation:** immediate selection feedback, 850 ms resolution beats, damage numbers, short camera shake and clear turn order. Visible encounters let the player decide when to engage.

**Scope:** compact four-encounter chapter. No random encounters, shops, party composition menu or grinding. XP and gold celebrate progress; they do not imply an unfinished leveling or economy system.
